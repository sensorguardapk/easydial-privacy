How Easy Dial uses information and Android permissions.

Easy Dial ("the App") is an Android calling companion published by SensorGuard. It helps users choose between cellular calling, WhatsApp, and WhatsApp Business while continuing to use their existing phone application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Easy Dial may access or process, how that information is used, when it may be shared with other services, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Access and Process

Phone Numbers and Calling Choices

When you enter or select a telephone number, Easy Dial may read the displayed number, normalize its country code, and process the calling or messaging option you select. This allows Easy Dial to perform the specific action requested by you.

Saved Contacts

If you grant Contacts permission, Easy Dial may access saved contact names and telephone numbers to assist with identifying the intended recipient and initiating WhatsApp calling.

The App uses contact lookup functionality for its calling features. Access to Contacts can be revoked in Android Settings. Revoking this permission may limit calling assistance for saved contacts.

Accessibility Information

Easy Dial uses an optional Android Accessibility Service to recognize relevant information displayed in supported phone and messaging applications.

Depending on the screen, Accessibility information may include visible telephone numbers, contact names, interface labels, accessibility element identifiers, and screen or window changes.

When you initiate a WhatsApp voice call through Easy Dial, the Accessibility Service may perform limited user-requested interactions inside WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, including opening the relevant conversation and selecting the Voice Call option.

Accessibility access is technically capable of exposing sensitive on-screen information. Easy Dial is intended to use that access only for the calling assistance described here. The App is not designed to collect message conversations, passwords, PINs, biometric credentials, or call audio.

Easy Dial is a general calling companion, not an application specifically designed as a disability assistance tool.

WhatsApp App Lock

If WhatsApp requires biometric, PIN, or password authentication, Easy Dial may detect that the application is awaiting unlock and temporarily pause a pending call request.

You complete authentication through WhatsApp or Android's own security interface. Easy Dial does not authenticate on your behalf, capture your authentication credentials, or bypass application locks. Pending actions are subject to time limits and cancellation.

Application Preferences

Easy Dial may store preferences locally on your device, including your country calling code, preferred WhatsApp application, and optional "Ask How to Call" setting.

The App also checks whether relevant Android permissions and roles have been enabled so it can determine which features are available.

Diagnostic Information

Android system logs and development or testing versions of Easy Dial may contain technical events, application state information, and potentially telephone numbers or contact names.

Such information may be accessible to someone with authorized debugging access to the device. Users should review diagnostic logs before sharing them with support.

2. Android Permissions

Easy Dial may request the following permissions and capabilities:

  • Contacts Permission: Used to look up saved contact numbers and names for calling assistance.
  • Accessibility Service: Used to recognize relevant on-screen information and perform user-requested calling interactions in supported applications.
  • Display Over Other Apps: Used to display contextual calling shortcuts over the existing phone keypad.
  • Call Redirection Role: An optional Android role used when you enable "Ask How to Call." It allows Easy Dial to present alternative calling options when you attempt to place a cellular call. Depending on your selection, the original cellular call may proceed, be redirected where supported, or be canceled.
  • Android Calling Capabilities: Where necessary and permitted, used to initiate the cellular calling action that you explicitly select.

Permissions are controlled by Android. You may review or revoke permissions through your device settings. Certain app features may stop working if required permissions are disabled.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed or processed by Easy Dial is used for:

  • Recognizing telephone numbers entered in the phone keypad.
  • Displaying available calling and messaging choices.
  • Identifying the intended calling recipient.
  • Initiating user-requested WhatsApp or cellular calling actions.
  • Maintaining calling preferences and app settings.
  • Supporting technical troubleshooting when users request help.

4. Sharing Information With Other Services

When you choose to contact someone through WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, Easy Dial may pass the selected destination telephone number or conversation target to the relevant WhatsApp application through an Android intent or compatible link.

WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business are independently operated services. Their collection and processing of information are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

When you select cellular calling, Android's phone services and your mobile network operator process the call according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Easy Dial does not control the processing performed independently by WhatsApp, Google, Android service providers, or telecommunications operators.

Information You Send to Support

If you contact SensorGuard by email, we may receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you voluntarily provide.

This information is used to respond to questions, investigate reported problems, and process privacy-related requests. Email service providers may process these communications according to their own privacy policies.

Please do not send passwords, contact lists, authentication codes, or sensitive diagnostic information in support messages.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Easy Dial may store its calling preferences and application settings locally on your Android device.

During a user-requested calling action, Easy Dial may temporarily process a destination number, saved contact information, or relevant accessibility data needed to complete that action.

Local app preferences ordinarily remain until changed, the App's storage is cleared, or the App is uninstalled. Android system logs and other installed applications may maintain information separately.

If you contact SensorGuard, we may retain your correspondence for as long as necessary to address the request or support issue, or as required by applicable law.

6. Your Privacy Choices

You can control Easy Dial's access to your device by:

  • Granting or denying Contacts permission.
  • Enabling or disabling Easy Dial's Accessibility Service.
  • Enabling or disabling permission to display over other apps.
  • Enabling or disabling the optional "Ask How to Call" feature.
  • Changing your preferred calling application and country code.
  • Clearing Easy Dial's local application data.
  • Uninstalling Easy Dial.

Disabling Easy Dial's permissions may limit its functionality. It does not remove your WhatsApp account or affect information independently held by your phone service provider.
